Pognon

A private expense tracker for iPhone. Your spending, your data: everything stays on your device.

Download on the App Store

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone only, encrypted by iOS. The app has no server, requires no account, and contains no ads or trackers.

How do I add an expense in three seconds?

With the + button in the app, the “New expense” widget on your Home or Lock Screen, or by telling Siri “Add an expense in Pognon”. In quick entry, the “Dictate” button understands “twelve fifty bakery”.

How do I scan a receipt?

In quick entry, tap the document icon at the top right, then take a photo, choose one, or import a PDF e-receipt. From Mail, Share → Pognon sends the receipt straight in. The amount, merchant, date and every line item are read on your iPhone, and the receipt stays attached to the expense.

What are “What I spend on” and Pognon's remarks?

Every receipt item is sorted into a family (everyday groceries, snacks, beauty, cleaning…). Based on those families and your budget pace, Pognon makes cheeky but encouraging remarks. Turn them off in Settings → Remarks and reminders.

What is “My month plan”?

The first card on the home screen. At first launch, or from “Let's plan your month”, you set in five steps what you earn, what you must pay, what you need and what you want to protect, with three paces to choose from (Flexible 90 / 10, Balanced 80 / 20, Ambitious 70 / 30) or an amount of your own. Your spending budget follows: income minus fixed costs minus the protected amount. The card then shows, to the cent, income received and expected, bills paid and upcoming, savings planned and done. If your income or bills change, Pognon offers to recompute and never touches the budget on its own.

What does “Budget still available” mean?

The second card: your spending budget minus what you have already spent and minus planned expenses, with the share of budget used against the share of the month gone, what that gives per day and for the next seven days. Fixed costs and installments are counted apart, never against this budget. It's a computed allowance, not your bank balance. Tap it to open “Where I stand”, the math laid out day by day. Without a plan, the “Today, you can still spend” card plays this role.

How do I handle fixed costs and income?

From “My month plan” → “Income and fixed bills”, or in quick entry with “Repeat”: rent, utilities, subscriptions, salary, benefits. Each month Pognon offers them in a sheet to confirm, or adds them automatically if you chose that. A fixed cost you already entered by hand is never counted twice. A bill that falls once a year (insurance, yearly subscription, taxes) is set to “Every year”: it counts for one twelfth each month in your plan and is proposed in full when its month comes, with no surprise for your budget.

Can I plan an expense for a future date?

Yes. In quick entry, pick a future date (up to a year ahead) and the button becomes “Plan”; the “+” on a future day in the calendar does the same. Nothing is spent before the date, but the amount is already taken off your available budget. The planned expense shows in the “Coming up” card on the home screen and in the calendar, then joins your expenses on the day.

What about a purchase paid in installments?

In quick entry, turn on “Pay in installments” and pick 2×, 3×, 4× or more: the amount you enter is the total, split to the cent. The first installment is added on the purchase day, the next ones on the same day of the following months, automatically, and they show up as planned in the calendar and in “Where I stand”.

I got a partial refund on a purchase. What now?

Open the expense, then “Refund part or all”. The net amount replaces the gross one in the category, the budget and the stats; the original expense and the refund stay visible. Beyond the expense amount, the excess is entered as income.

Can one supermarket receipt count in several categories?

Yes. After a scan, Pognon suggests a split based on the items: food in Groceries, detergent and light bulbs in Housing, shampoo in Shopping, medicine in Health. You adjust, then a single payment is saved with several parts. The mapping can be changed in Settings → Categories.

How do savings goals work?

A name, an amount, a date. With a month plan, the protected amount is split between your goals by priority and suggested every month with your fixed bills; without a plan, each goal follows its own pace; you can also round each expense up and deposit the difference with one tap, or set aside what was left from last month. Deposits are dated, milestones celebrated. Pognon moves no money: you set it aside, the app keeps count.

Can I import a statement from my bank?

Yes, with no bank login. Download the statement from your bank's website or app (PDF or CSV), or take a photo of the paper statement, even at an angle, then Settings → Backup → Import a bank statement. Everything is read on your iPhone: debits and credits recognized, labels cleaned up, expenses you already logged unchecked, transfers between your own accounts unchecked, reversals removed along with the transaction they cancel, categories suggested. Nothing enters Pognon until you confirm.

Does a shared expense count in full?

No. Turn on “Shared expense” and enter your share (÷2, ÷3, ÷4 with one tap): only your share counts toward your totals and budget.

Which notifications does Pognon send?

Two, both off by default and set in Settings → Remarks and reminders. The morning brief, at the time you choose: one line with what today can cost, the budget left and the bills to confirm. The evening reminder, only if nothing was noted that day, with two buttons, “No spending” and “Add”; it goes quiet after three days without an answer. Everything is computed on your iPhone.

How do I delete several entries at once?

Settings → Backup → Delete entries: a day, a week, a month, a period of your choice or everything, with the exact summary (count and total) before you confirm. Fixed bills, installment plans, goals and categories stay.

How do I lock the app?

Settings → Security → Lock with Face ID. The app will ask for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode every time it opens.

How do I back up or move to a new iPhone?

Your data is part of your iPhone backup. For a file of your own, Settings → Backup → Export my data creates a complete file to keep in Files or iCloud Drive, and “Restore a backup” reads it back. “Export as CSV” produces a spreadsheet for Numbers or Excel.

What costs money?

Logging expenses, the budget, income, the calendar, CSV export, backup and restore stay free, forever: your data is yours and must always be able to leave, even if you never pay. Pognon Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription, that supports development. What it unlocks is listed in Settings → Pognon Pro.

Which iPhone?

iPhone running iOS 26 or later.

Which languages?

English and French. The app follows your iPhone's language.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app: all its data goes with it, because nothing is stored anywhere else.
