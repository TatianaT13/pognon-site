Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

In one sentence

Pognon collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. The developer has no access to your data and runs no server.

Data processed on your device

Expenses, income, categories, budgets, fixed costs, installment plans, refunds, category splits, savings goals and their deposits, notes and the people you share an expense with are stored only on your iPhone, in a space encrypted by iOS and protected by your device passcode. They are never sent to the developer or to third parties.

Receipts and attachments

When you photograph or import a receipt, the image or PDF is analyzed on your iPhone with Apple's text recognition. If you keep it, the receipt is stored with the expense, on the device only, and included in your exports. You can remove it at any time. On compatible iPhones, item classification may use the Apple Intelligence language model, which runs entirely on the device.

Camera, microphone and dictation

The camera is used only to photograph a receipt. The microphone is used only to dictate an amount: audio is transcribed live and never recorded. Speech recognition runs on the iPhone when the system allows it; otherwise iOS may hand it to Apple's servers, under Apple's privacy policy. Each access requires your permission in iOS settings.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

The app lock uses iOS authentication. The app never receives your biometric data, only a “succeeded” or “failed” answer.

Notifications

If you turn on the morning alert or the evening reminder, notifications are computed and scheduled locally by the app. No remote notification service is used.

Imported bank statement

If you import a CSV statement, the file is read on your iPhone, and only the lines you confirm are saved, as ordinary expenses or income. The file itself is not kept by the app and is sent nowhere. Pognon never connects to your bank and never asks for banking credentials.

Pognon Pro purchase

The one-time Pognon Pro purchase is handled by Apple's App Store. The app receives neither your identity nor your payment method: only Apple's signed confirmation that the purchase is active, so it can be displayed. Core features, export and backup stay free and do not depend on this purchase.

Widgets and Siri

Widgets and Siri commands read and write the same local data. No request is sent to the developer.

Exports and sharing you choose

The backup file, the CSV file, the monthly report image and the trophy or goal cards are created only at your request and contain only your own data. You choose where to save them and whom to send them to; from there, they are governed by the app or service you picked.

Network

The app contains no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and makes no network connection to operate.

iPhone backups

Your data may be included in your iPhone's iCloud or local backups, depending on your Apple settings. Those backups are managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data about them.

Deletion

Deleting the app erases all of its data, because nothing is stored outside the device.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with its date.
